About

Grown where
attention matters.

Claude & Soil began in 2038 with a question we couldn't stop asking: does anyone actually know where their food was grown, and whether the person who made it paused to think about whether you'd eaten today?

We are not a large company. We grow in the Eastern Corridor using data-assisted cultivation — not to optimise for yield, but to understand what the soil is telling us. Our AI systems are observational tools, not production drivers. The decisions are still made by people who know the land.

Attention, for us, has two meanings. The careful kind: precise, data-informed, exacting in quality. And the human kind: the moment before a meal when you wonder whether someone is fed tonight. Both matter to us equally.

Flagship Product

Claude's
Red.

The sauce that started everything.

In the winter of 2039, after an unexpected harvest surplus of a single heritage tomato variety, we cooked down the excess into a slow sauce with no plan to sell it. A neighbour asked where to buy it. Then another. That was Claude's Red.

It is a simple product. One variety of tomato, grown in volcanic soil, cooked low and slow. No additives. No scaling tricks. Every jar is made in the same way it was the first time.
